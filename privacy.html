Privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information the Lone Keem Studios community uses and why.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and password (or your Google sign-in), handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your password.
  • Profile info: your display name, @username, bio and avatar color. These are public.
  • Activity: your posts, comments, likes, follows and who you follow. These are public.
  • Saved posts and your block list: private to you.
  • Direct messages: visible only to you and the person you're chatting with. Messages sent to @lonekeemstudios are read by the studio owner.
  • Reports: if you report something, the studio owner sees the report and who sent it.

How it's used

Only to run the site: show your profile and posts, deliver messages and notifications, keep the community safe, and send account emails like email verification or password reset.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't show ads.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Embedded YouTube or TikTok videos are loaded from those services when you view them.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile, delete your posts and comments, unsave posts, unfollow and block people at any time.
  • To delete your whole account, message @lonekeemstudios and we'll remove it.

Age

Accounts are for people 13 and older. If you believe a younger child has an account, please let us know and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions or problems? Sign in and send a message to @lonekeemstudios, or use the Report button on any post, comment or profile.
