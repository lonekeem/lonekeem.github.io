Privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information Kemy (by Lone Keem Studios) and the Lone Keem Studios community use and why.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and password (or your Google sign-in), handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your password.
  • Profile info: your display name, @username (and your account ID), bio, pronouns, website, avatar color and avatar photo. These are public.
  • Activity: your posts, captions, hashtags, comments (which can include emoji, a photo stored on Cloudinary, a GIF from GIPHY or a sticker of your Pal), gold stars (likes), follows, and the views and plays you add to posts. These are public or shown as counts.
  • GIFs: GIF search and GIFs in comments load straight from GIPHY, so GIPHY receives your search words, IP address and browser details under GIPHY's privacy policy.
  • Photos and videos: photo and video posts, stories, snaps and your avatar photo, uploaded to our media host, Cloudinary.
  • Community Movies and Songs: movie files, audio files, posters and cover art you upload are stored with Cloudinary. Titles, descriptions, lyrics, genres, ratings, “AI tools used” and YouTube links are stored in Cloud Firestore and are public.
  • Games: game records, scores, wins, leaderboards and online match moves are stored in Cloud Firestore. Kemy’s Bubble Quest keeps your best times on your device, and in Firestore (for the leaderboard) when you’re signed in.
  • Kemy Pals: your Pal’s name, species, look, care stats, XP and visits are stored in Cloud Firestore and shown on your profile.
  • Saved posts and your block list: private to you.
  • Direct messages, live chat, story replies and game quick chat: direct messages are visible only to you and the person you’re chatting with. Messages sent to @lonekeemstudios are read by the studio owner. Live chat is visible to everyone in that live.
  • Reports: if you report something, the studio owner sees the report and who sent it.
  • Search history: your recent searches are kept in your browser and, when you are signed in, in a private part of your account that only you can read (up to 20 searches), so they follow you to other devices. Remove one with ✕ or tap Clear all in Search to delete them, including the copy in your account. They are deleted with your account.
  • Trending searches: when you are signed in, a family-friendly search you submit may add one to a shared, anonymous daily counter for that search phrase (for example “kidz bop: 5 today”). It does not record who searched. Searches with blocked words are never counted.
  • Optional YouTube search: if the site owner turns on the “More from YouTube” row, your search words are sent to YouTube (Google) to find videos, under Google's privacy policy. It is off by default.

Safety scanning on your device

Before a photo, video, caption or comment is posted, Kemy checks it for unsafe content (like nudity or blocked words) right on your device. The scan itself doesn't send your media anywhere; only what you choose to post is uploaded.

Camera and microphone

Kemy uses your camera and microphone only while you film a photo or video, add a story or snap, or go live, and only after your browser asks you for permission. Filters and face effects run on your device. Nothing from your camera is saved unless you choose to post it.

Live streams

Kemy lives are sent peer-to-peer (WebRTC, always encrypted) straight from the host to viewers. Live video and audio are not recorded or stored on any server. Only the live’s title, viewer count, gold stars, chat messages and reports are stored in Cloud Firestore. If a host links a YouTube live, it plays in an embedded YouTube player, and YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to it.

Kemy Pals and Talk to your Pal

Your Pals, their outfits, Star Coins, items and Pal Games results are stored in Cloud Firestore. Star Coins are free in-app points: they can’t be bought or cashed out. In Pal Park, other players in your room see your display name, @handle, your Pal and where you walk, plus any emotes or chat bubbles you send; your position is removed when you leave.

Talk to your Pal is an AI chat. When you send a message, the message, a short recent chat history (about the last 10 messages), your Pal’s name, species, level and stats, your @handle and any small facts you told your Pal are sent to Pollinations.AI (text.pollinations.ai), a free third-party AI service, to write the reply; their own privacy policy applies to that request. No account details, email or location are sent. Messages that look unsafe (for example sexual, violent, self-harm or personal-info topics) are not sent at all and get a safe built-in reply instead. Your recent Pal chat is saved in your account (only you can read it) and you can erase it any time with “Forget our chat”. If you tap the mic, your browser’s speech recognition turns your voice into text (on some browsers, such as Chrome, this uses the browser maker’s speech service); Kemy doesn’t record or store audio. Pal voices are made on your device. AI can make mistakes, so please don’t share personal information with your Pal.

Feed ranking, YouTube videos and news headlines

For You ranking happens on your device. To pick what to show you, Kemy keeps simple interest scores (for example “likes Funny and Dance, watches this creator to the end”) based on what you watch, skip, star, comment on, share and save, the topics you pick, and what you search for and tap in search results. You can see, remove or reset them in Settings › Your interests. They are stored in your browser and, when you are signed in, in a private part of your account that only you can read. They are never shown to anyone else or used for ads, and they are deleted with your account. Shares are counted once per account.

YouTube videos in the Funny, News and Dance tabs come from a short, hand-picked list of public, family-friendly channels and are labelled “From YouTube”. We don’t copy or re-upload them. Until you tap Watch, only the video’s thumbnail image is loaded from YouTube (i.ytimg.com). When you tap Watch, the video plays in YouTube’s own player from youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode), and YouTube/Google’s privacy policy applies to that player. YouTube’s own safety systems apply to the videos; we also skip titles with mature words.

News headlines come from the public RSS feeds of reputable outlets (such as BBC Newsround, Science News Explores and NASA), are labelled with the source name and open the original story on that outlet’s website, where its own privacy policy applies. The list is refreshed every few hours by an automatic job; no information about you is sent to these sources by Kemy.

How it's used

Only to run the app: show your profile, posts, movies, songs and games, deliver messages and notifications, keep the community safe, and send account emails like email verification or password reset.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, we don't share it with advertisers, and we don't show ads. Kemy has no ad, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and doesn't collect your location or contacts.

Where it's stored

Account data, posts, messages, game data and Pals are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Photos, videos and audio are stored with Cloudinary. Both act as service providers for us. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and everything is sent over HTTPS. The Kemy Android app opens this same site and collects nothing extra. Embedded YouTube or TikTok players load from those services only when you play them.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile, delete your posts, movies, songs and comments, unsave posts, unfollow and block people at any time.
  • Delete your account any time in the app from Edit profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile and @handle, your posts, movies, songs and stories (with their comments, stars and views), your comments on other posts, your follows and followers, saved posts, blocks, notifications, live sessions, your Kemy Pals, your message threads and your login.
  • A few small things can stay: the star and view counts you added to other people's posts (they no longer link to a profile) reports you sent, which we keep for safety, and your past game matches, game stats and Bubble Quest leaderboard times. Email us (below) and we’ll remove those too.
  • Media removal: photos, videos and audio you uploaded stay on Cloudinary after you delete your account, but they no longer appear anywhere in the app. To have the files erased, email lillykemoy1@gmail.com with your @handle.

Parental Controls

A parent can turn on Parental Controls in Settings with a 4-digit parent PIN. The PIN itself is never stored: we keep only a salted SHA-256 hash in a private record that only the account can read. The choices (chat, messages, going live, comments, purchases, daily time limit, profile visibility and restricted mode) are saved with the profile so our security rules can block chat, messages, comments and going live when they are off. Daily screen time is counted only on the device. A forgotten PIN can be reset by signing in to the account again.

Age

Accounts are for people 13 and older. If you believe a younger child has an account, please let us know and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions or problems? Sign in and send a message to @lonekeemstudios, or use the Report button on any post, comment or profile. You can also email us at lillykemoy@yahoo.com, including to ask for your account or data to be deleted.
