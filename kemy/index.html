Kemy Pals: your own virtual pet

Hatch an egg and meet your own virtual pet. Choose from 30 cute species, give your Pal a name, and dress it up with colours, patterns, hats, hoodies, dresses and capes. Feed it snacks, give it a bubbly shower, brush it, tuck it in at bedtime and play catch. Care earns XP, so your Pal glows up through three forms.

Your Pal talks back in cute voices when you chat with it, hangs out on your profile, naps at night and reacts when friends stop by to give it a pat. Walk it around Pal Park to find treasures and make friends, and challenge friends or the computer in Pal Games: Pal Race, Bubble Pop Battle, Star Catch and Pal Dance-Off. Earn free Star Coins for outfits and room items. Nothing costs real money.

AI Movie Maker: make a short movie from one idea

Type an idea like “two robots share an umbrella in the rain”. Kemy writes a script, paints the backgrounds and characters, and brings them to life as animated 2D puppets that walk, blink, talk and gesture. Shots get camera moves, weather and particles, each character gets its own voice with captions, and music and titles are added for you. Tweak any shot, then post the finished video to Community Movies or turn it into a series with numbered episodes. A Storybook mode makes narrated picture-book movies too. It's free and runs in your browser.

Free games

Play a friend online or play against the computer. All games are free.

  • Kemy's Bubble Quest

    A cute side-scrolling platformer: hop, swim and blow bubbles with Kemy the axolotl across 8 worlds and a secret bonus world. 1 player

  • Star Pool

    Gold rails and purple felt. Sink your group of balls, then call the 8. Play a friend or the computer. 2 players

  • Chess

    Gold against purple with full rules and optional clocks, online or against the computer. 2 players

  • Ludo

    Roll fair dice and race your four tokens home with 2 to 4 players. 2-4 players

  • Checkers

    Jump, crown and clear the board. 2 players

  • Star Drop

    Drop gold and purple stars; four in a row wins. 2 players

  • Star Cards

    A colourful shedding card game for 2 to 6 players. Shout LAST STAR! at one card. 2-6 players

  • Studio Trivia

    A fast party quiz about music, movies and more for up to 8 players. 2-8 players

  • KL Grid

    K against L on a 3x3 grid, or go big on the Ultimate 9x9 board. 2 players

Frequently asked questions

What is Kemy?

Kemy is the free community app from Lone Keem Studios. You can raise your own Kemy Pal virtual pet, make short movies with the free AI Movie Maker, play free games like Kemy's Bubble Quest, Star Pool, Chess and Ludo, and watch and share community movies, songs, funny videos, stories and live streams.

Does Kemy have a virtual pet?

Yes. Kemy Pals is the virtual pet built into Kemy. Hatch an egg, choose from 30 cute species, give your Pal a name and outfits, then feed it, give it bubbly showers, brush it, tuck it in at bedtime and play with it. Your Pal talks back in cute voices, visits Pal Park and plays Pal Games with friends. It is free.

Is Kemy free?

Yes. Kemy is free to use. There is nothing to buy to play the games, raise a Pal or make an AI movie.

How do I get Kemy on my phone?

Open lonekeem.github.io/app.html in your browser and choose Install or Add to Home Screen. On Android, Kemy by Lone Keem Studios is also coming to Google Play.

What is a Kemy Pal?

A Kemy Pal is your own virtual pet. Hatch it from an egg, pick one of 30 species, give it a name, colours and outfits, then feed, shower, brush and play with it to help it grow through three glow-up forms. It talks back in cute voices, lives on your profile, visits Pal Park and plays Pal Games.

How does the AI Movie Maker work?

Type an idea and Kemy writes a short script, paints the scenes, animates the characters with voices and captions, adds music and edits it into a video you can post to Community Movies or a series. It is free and runs in your browser.

Which games can I play in Kemy?

Kemy's Bubble Quest, Star Pool, Chess, Ludo, Checkers, Star Drop, Star Cards, Studio Trivia and KL Grid. Most games work online with friends or against the computer.

Who makes Kemy?

Kemy is made by Lone Keem Studios, the studio founded by Lone Keem, who also writes the studio's original songs and AI music videos.

Is Kemy safe for my family?

Kemy has community guidelines, reporting and blocking, age ratings on movies and songs, and content filters. Read the Community Guidelines and Privacy Policy linked at the bottom of this page.
