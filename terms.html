Terms of Use

Last updated October 19, 2026

These are the basic rules for using the Lone Keem Studios website and community features (accounts, posts, comments, stars (likes), saves, follows, live streams and messages). By creating an account or posting, you agree to them.

Who can join

You must be 13 years old or older to create an account.

Your account

  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, including Lone Keem Studios.
  • One person, one account. Don't create accounts to get around a block or removal.

What you post

  • Only post things you made or have the right to share. No stolen content.
  • No explicit, sexual, or graphic content, and nothing hateful, threatening, or harassing.
  • Follow the Community Guidelines.
  • You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you let Lone Keem Studios show it on this site.
  • Live streams follow these same rules. Only go live with things you have the right to show. Lone Keem Studios may end a live that breaks them. Lives aren't recorded, but people watching can see and screenshot what you share. A big live with YouTube must be your own YouTube stream; YouTube's own terms apply to it too.

Studio content

The movies, music and music videos from Lone Keem Studios belong to the studio. Please don't download, re-upload or sell them.

Removing content and accounts

The owner of Lone Keem Studios can remove any post, comment or account at any time, for example when it breaks these rules or the Community Guidelines.

You can delete your own account at any time from Edit profile → Delete account. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone. See the Privacy Policy for what's removed and how to have uploaded media files erased.

No guarantees

This is a small, independent studio site. Features may change, pause or stop, and the site may sometimes be unavailable.

Changes

These terms may be updated. If you keep using the site after a change, the new version applies.

Contact

Questions or problems? Sign in and send a message to @lonekeemstudios, or use the Report button on any post, comment or profile.
