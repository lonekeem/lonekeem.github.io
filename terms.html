Terms of Use

Last updated October 22, 2026

These are the basic rules for using Kemy (by Lone Keem Studios), the Lone Keem Studios website and community features (accounts, posts, comments, stars (likes), saves, follows, live streams and messages). By creating an account or posting, you agree to them.

Who can join

Kemy asks for a real birthday. Players 13 and older can post, comment, and use filtered public chat. Players under 13, and accounts with no birthday yet, can watch and play with stricter limits: phrase chat only, no public posts or uploads, a private profile, and no friend requests. The birthday is typed by the player. It is not an ID check and not a parent-consent form. Kemy does not hold a kids-privacy certification. Details are on the Kids safety and parents page.

Your account

  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, including Lone Keem Studios.
  • One person, one account. Don't create accounts to get around a block or removal.

What you post

  • Only post things you made or have the right to share. No stolen content.
  • No explicit, sexual, or graphic content, and nothing hateful, threatening, or harassing.
  • Follow the Community Guidelines.
  • You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you let Lone Keem Studios show it on this site.
  • Live streams follow these same rules. Only go live with things you have the right to show. Lone Keem Studios may end a live that breaks them. Lives aren't recorded, but people watching can see and screenshot what you share. A big live with YouTube must be your own YouTube stream; YouTube's own terms apply to it too.

Movies

Share only movies you made, or have the rights to share. No pirated or copyrighted films, trailers or clips from other studios. Every community movie is labeled AI-made; say which AI tools you used when you can. Pick an honest age rating (All ages, 13+, 16+ or 18+). 18+ movies are blurred until a viewer confirms their age, and anything sexual or violent must be rated 18+ or not posted at all.

Songs

Share only songs and beats you made, or have the rights to share. Don’t upload other artists’ songs, covers you don’t have rights to, or samples you can’t clear. Every community song is labeled AI-made. Mark explicit lyrics with the E toggle.

Studio content

The movies, music and music videos from Lone Keem Studios belong to the studio. Please don't download, re-upload or sell them.

Removing content and accounts

The owner of Lone Keem Studios can remove any post, comment or account at any time, for example when it breaks these rules or the Community Guidelines.

You can delete your account and Firestore data at any time with Delete my account and data in Settings or Edit profile. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone. See the Privacy Policy for what is removed, what can remain, and how to ask for uploaded media files to be erased.

No guarantees

This is a small, independent studio site. Features may change, pause or stop, and the site may sometimes be unavailable.

Changes

These terms may be updated. If you keep using the site after a change, the new version applies.

Chat, reports, and ads

Players cannot privately message each other. Public chat blocks links, phone numbers, emails, other-app invites, and questions that ask for personal details. Block and Report are on player names and chat lines. Reports go to the moderation panel with recent chat. There are no ads and no ad trackers.

Contact

Questions or problems? Sign in and use Message the Kemy team. Only the studio owner reads those notes. You can also use Report or Block on a player, a chat line, a post, or a comment.
