Add Kemy to your home screen: full screen, fast, and free.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button ⬆️ at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Kemy opens full screen from your home screen.

What you can do in the Kemy app

Kemy Pals virtual pet. Hatch your own Pal from 30 cute species, name it and dress it up, then feed it, shower it, brush it, tuck it in at bedtime and play. It talks back in cute voices, glows up as it levels, visits Pal Park and plays Pal Games with friends.

AI Movie Maker. Type one idea and Kemy writes, paints, animates, voices and edits a short movie for you, free, right in your browser. Post it to Community Movies or start a series.

Free games. Kemy’s Bubble Quest, Star Pool, Chess, Ludo, Checkers, Star Drop, Star Cards, Studio Trivia and KL Grid. Play friends online or the computer.

Community movies and songs. Watch and share movies and music, follow series, give stars and comment, plus a video feed with filters, stories and live streams.
