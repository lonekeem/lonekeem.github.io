Community Guidelines

Last updated October 26, 2026

Kemy (by Lone Keem Studios) is a place where AI meets imagination. Keep it creative, kind and safe for everyone. These guidelines are part of the Terms of Service. They apply to everything on Kemy: posts, comments, stories, profiles, movies, songs, live streams, live chat, game and Pal Park chat, Pal names, houses and usernames.

Do

  • Share your own creations: ideas, AI art, music, videos and links to your work.
  • Give honest, respectful feedback.
  • Credit the tools and people who helped you make something.
  • Pick an honest age rating for what you post.
  • Keep personal info private: yours and everyone else’s.

Don't

  • No stolen content. Don't post other people's work as your own or re-upload studio releases.
  • No nudity, explicit or sexual content, and no real violence or gore.
  • No harassment, bullying, hate, threats, or sharing someone's private information.
  • No spam, scams, or fake accounts, and no pretending to be Lone Keem Studios or anyone else.
  • No content involving anyone under 18 in a sexual or unsafe way. Ever.
  • No promoting self-harm, dangerous challenges, illegal drugs, weapons, or anything illegal.
  • No cheating, exploiting bugs, or trying to get around filters, age limits or blocks.
  • No selling or trading accounts, Star Coins, Diamonds or items for real money.
  • No arranging to meet people from Kemy in person.

Age ratings for creators

When you post, you choose who can see it: All ages (the default), 13+ only, or 17+ only. You can change it later from the post’s menu with Age limit. Viewers whose birthday says they are too young never see a 13+ or 17+ post. Signed-out viewers, and accounts with no birthday saved, have to confirm “I’m 13+” or “I’m 17+” first. Grids show those posts blurred with a 13+ or 17+ badge.

You are responsible for choosing the right rating. If you aren’t sure, pick the higher one. A rating is a heads-up for viewers, not a pass to break the rules.

All ages

Fine for anyone, including younger players: everyday life, art, music, dancing, pets, games, comedy without mean jokes, and cartoon action with no one really hurt.

13+ only

  • Mild language that isn’t aimed at anyone.
  • Spooky or scary themes, jump scares, and fantasy or cartoon fighting with no blood.
  • Teen topics, like school stress or crushes, talked about respectfully and with nothing sexual.
  • News and real-world topics that may upset younger kids, shared without graphic detail.

17+ only

  • Stronger language used now and then, never as an insult, slur or attack.
  • Intense fictional action or horror themes, without realistic blood, injuries or gore.
  • Serious, mature topics, such as mental health, loss, or tough news, discussed thoughtfully and without graphic detail or instructions.

Never allowed, at any rating

  • Nudity, sexual content, sexual jokes or sexual comments, or anything sexualizing a minor.
  • Real violence, real gore, injuries, animal cruelty, or shocking footage.
  • Hate, bullying, harassment, threats, or attacks on any person or group.
  • Promoting self-harm, eating disorders, dangerous challenges, drugs, alcohol for minors, or weapons.
  • Scams, spam, stolen content, private information, or anything illegal.

Lone Keem Studios and its moderators can change a post’s rating, limit it, or remove it. Rating something lower than it should be on purpose, again and again, counts as breaking these guidelines.

Live streams

The same rules apply when you go live, in live chat, and to the stars you send. Lone Keem Studios can end any live that breaks these guidelines. Kemy lives aren't recorded or saved by Kemy, but viewers can still screenshot them.

Going live with YouTube? The stream must be from your own YouTube channel, and it has to follow these guidelines (and YouTube's rules) too. A YouTube live is public on YouTube, or unlisted if you choose.

Movies

Share only movies you made, or have the rights to share. No pirated or copyrighted films, trailers or clips from other studios. Every community movie is labeled AI-made; say which AI tools you used when you can. Pick an honest movie rating (All ages, 13+, 16+ or 18+). Intense fictional action or scary scenes need 16+ or 18+. 18+ movies are blurred until a viewer confirms their age. Nudity, sexual content and real gore are never allowed, at any rating.

Songs

Share only songs and beats you made, or have the rights to share. Don’t upload other artists’ songs, covers you don’t have rights to, or samples you can’t clear. Every community song is labeled AI-made. Mark explicit lyrics with the E toggle. Lyrics follow the same rules as posts: no sexual lyrics, hate or threats.

Chat

Players can’t privately message each other. Public chat in games and the world stays on, with a word filter and Kemy Patrol. If you are under 13, or we don’t know your age, chat is a list of friendly phrases. Links, phone numbers, emails, and invites to other apps aren’t allowed. Asking someone’s age, address, or school, asking if they are alone, asking for a picture, or saying not to tell a parent is blocked, and moderators are told.

Age

Enter your real birthday. Players 13 and older can post and use filtered chat. Younger players, and accounts with no birthday saved, use phrase chat, cannot post or upload, have a private profile, and cannot send friend requests. They also never see posts marked 13+ or 17+. A parent PIN does not remove those limits. Kemy does not claim a kids-privacy certification. Read Kids safety and parents.

Reporting and blocking

See something that breaks these guidelines, or a post with the wrong age rating? Use Report and Block on a player’s name, a chat line, a post, a comment, or a profile. Reports go to the moderation panel with the recent chat. A chat line is hidden after enough different people report it. You can also Block someone so they can't follow you or comment on your posts. Please only report real problems: if many of your reports aren’t, reporting can be paused for a while.

What happens if you break the rules

The owner of Lone Keem Studios and Kemy’s moderators review reports and posts the safety check holds. Depending on how serious it is, and whether it has happened before, we may:

  • Remove or re-rate the post, comment, story or live, or hold your new posts for review before they appear.
  • Warn you (a strike). Warnings are recorded, and more of them lead to stronger steps.
  • Pause features such as posting, comments, stories, going live, or reporting.
  • Suspend your account for a set time.
  • Ban your account permanently and remove it. New accounts made to get around a ban are banned too.
  • Mark the account as possibly under 13, which turns on the under-13 limits.
  • Report it to the authorities when someone may be in danger or the law requires it.

Serious harm, such as anything sexual involving a minor, grooming, threats of violence, or sharing someone’s private information, can lead to an immediate permanent ban with no warning. We can act with or without warning, and the Terms of Service explain more. If you think we got it wrong, reply to the moderation notice or use Message the Kemy team.

Contact

Questions or problems? Sign in and choose Message the Kemy team (feedback or a bug). Only the Kemy owner reads those notes. You can also use the Report button on any post, comment or profile.
