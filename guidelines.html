Community Guidelines

Last updated October 19, 2026

Lone Keem Studios is a place where AI meets imagination. Keep it creative, kind and safe for everyone.

Do

  • Share your own creations: ideas, AI art, music, videos and links to your work.
  • Give honest, respectful feedback.
  • Credit the tools and people who helped you make something.

Don't

  • No stolen content. Don't post other people's work as your own or re-upload studio releases.
  • No explicit or sexual content, and no graphic violence or gore.
  • No harassment, bullying, hate, threats, or sharing someone's private information.
  • No spam, scams, or fake accounts, and no pretending to be Lone Keem Studios or anyone else.
  • No content involving anyone under 18 in a sexual or unsafe way. Ever.

Live streams

The same rules apply when you go live, in live chat, and to the stars you send. Lone Keem Studios can end any live that breaks these guidelines. Lives aren't recorded or saved.

Going live with YouTube? The stream must be from your own YouTube channel, and it has to follow these guidelines (and YouTube's rules) too.

Age

You must be 13 or older to have an account.

Reporting and blocking

See something that breaks these guidelines? Use Report on the post, comment or profile. You can also Block anyone so they can't follow you, comment on your posts or message you.

What happens next

The owner of Lone Keem Studios reviews reports and can remove any content or account that breaks these guidelines, with or without warning.

Contact

Questions or problems? Sign in and send a message to @lonekeemstudios, or use the Report button on any post, comment or profile.
